Your location — your unique air

Public maps rely on a few monitoring stations, often near major roads or in other parts of the city. Air quality, noise, temperature and humidity change from one street to another. Altruist Urban measures the environment where you actually live.

Meet air quality monitor Altruist Urban

Years of environmental monitoring experience shaped a device built to help you understand the real conditions around your home — not generalized city averages.

Your data, your hardware, your choice

Read your data in three apps, or build your own open-source solution. With open protocols and open-source software, the sensor isn't locked to a single platform, cloud or vendor.

  1. Altruist.local

    The web app built into the device, on your Wi-Fi network. No servers involved.

  2. Home Assistant

    Integration with one of the most popular smart home platforms. Needs a local Home Assistant server.

  3. sensors.social

    Community map with extended analytics and history, built on Robonomics, an open-source cloud rather than a single vendor's.

## Why we went

Altruist is built to sit on a balcony and quietly measure the air, day and night. We wanted to see how it copes away from home: in hotel rooms, on busy streets, on a tropical island and on the window sills of skyscrapers. So we packed a few sensors and filmed the trip as a series of short episodes.

## Bangkok: two streets, two different airs

The tour started in Bangkok. One sensor went next to a big avenue, another into an apartment on a quiet side street a few minutes' walk away. Almost the same point on the map, yet the readings and the rhythm of the day were clearly different.

Two more surprises. The air next to a highway with a train running overhead was cleaner than we expected. And while the air on the main avenues was often fine, the noise was not. That is why Altruist measures sound as well as dust: a calm street is part of a healthy one.

## Koh Phangan: smoke in paradise

On Koh Phangan we spread sensors across several spots on the island and let them run for days. Then someone nearby started burning waste. The closest sensors caught the jump in fine particles at once, and it showed up on the sensors.social map, where anyone in the area could see it and react: close the windows, step away, raise the issue.

## Hong Kong: street to skyline

In Hong Kong we measured on bustling streets, in cafés, in parks and high up in towers. Every reading went into an open, tamper-proof public record. The same live data can drive automations in Home Assistant, for example turning on a purifier when the air outside gets worse.

## Guangzhou: the sensor keeps working while you rest

The finale was in Guangzhou. Most of the experiments were done, so we mostly enjoyed the view from the window. Altruist did not take a break: city or home, it keeps measuring even while you sleep.

## What we take home

- Air is local. A city average says little about your street, your floor or your room.
- Noise matters. Some of the loudest places had perfectly breathable air.
- Open data helps everyone nearby. One sensor can warn a whole neighbourhood about smoke.
- Setup should be easy anywhere. Every new episode ends with a QR code: scan it, pick your AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok or another) and it walks you through setup in your own language.
